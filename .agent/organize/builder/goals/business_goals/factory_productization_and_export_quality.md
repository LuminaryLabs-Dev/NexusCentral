# Business Goal: Factory Productization And Export Quality

- `business_goal_id`: `factory_productization_and_export_quality`
- `priority`: `high`
- `goal_statement`: Move procedural factory repos from technical demos toward commercially useful, export-ready production workflows.
- `why_it_matters`: NexusBotanyFactory, NexusAviationFactory, NexusTextureFactory, NexusFactoryFactory, NexusWorldFactory, NexusIdeaFactory, NexusCelestialFactory, and NexusHardwareFactory all center on authored outputs, but their export, packaging, validation, or catalog quality still requires deliberate planning.
- `supporting_projects`: `NexusBotanyFactory`, `NexusAviationFactory`, `NexusTextureFactory`, `NexusFactoryFactory`, `NexusWorldFactory`, `NexusIdeaFactory`, `NexusCelestialFactory`, `NexusHardwareFactory`.
- `manual_effort_reduced`: Repeated export reviews, packaging notes, template audits, and product-gap planning.
- `suggested_workflow_focus`: Export-readiness reviews, template governance, setup automation, and reusable product-gap documentation.
