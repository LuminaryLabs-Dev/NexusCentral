# Business Goal: Structured Content Parity And Traceability

- `business_goal_id`: `structured_content_parity_and_traceability`
- `priority`: `high`
- `goal_statement`: Keep scene maps, normalized content indexes, mirrored docs, and structured evidence traceable while projects are migrated, mirrored, or rebuilt.
- `why_it_matters`: `MuseumMultiverse` now carries a docs-first parity layer against `MM_GDoCExpoDemo18`, while `LuminaryInitiatives` and `LuminaryWiki` continue to depend on normalized records and source traceability. Without a dedicated planning layer, scene gaps, mirror drift, and content lineage become expensive to re-derive.
- `supporting_projects`: `MuseumMultiverse`, `MM_GDoCExpoDemo18`, `LuminaryInitiatives`, `LuminaryWiki`, `ProjectArrowhead-Demo`, `NexusBotanyFactory`.
- `manual_effort_reduced`: Repeated scene mapping, counterpart-gap reporting, source-of-truth comparison, mirror-drift explanation, and structured evidence packet authoring.
- `suggested_workflow_focus`: Content-parity reviews, migration evidence briefs, source-traceability reports, and rebuild-target packet seeding.
