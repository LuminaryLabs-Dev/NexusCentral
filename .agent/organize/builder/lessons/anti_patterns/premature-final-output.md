# Premature Final Output

Date: `2026-03-25`

## Anti-Pattern

Treating polished final outputs as the starting deliverable before the workflow inventory, lifecycle placement, and support templates are reviewed.

## Why It Hurts

- It hides unresolved workflow gaps behind polished artifacts.
- It encourages content generation before the workspace has agreed rules for what should stay active, experimental, disabled, or archived.
- It makes later cleanup harder because output quality can distract from weak workflow structure.

## Corrective Action

Finish reconciliation first, record missing support gaps, and only then authorize final output generation.
