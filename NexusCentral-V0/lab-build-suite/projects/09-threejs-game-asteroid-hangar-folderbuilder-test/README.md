# Asteroid Hangar

A browser-playable 3D space game built with Three.js and Vite. Pilot your ship through a dangerous asteroid field, collect energy cells, and reach the docking ring to complete your mission.

## Features

- **3D Space Flight**: Smooth ship controls with physics-based movement
- **Asteroid Field**: Navigate through procedurally generated asteroids
- **Energy Management**: Collect energy cells to maintain your ship's power
- **Collision System**: Real-time collision detection with visual feedback
- **Camera Follow**: Dynamic third-person camera that tracks your ship
- **HUD Display**: Real-time score, energy, and progress tracking
- **Game States**: Start, pause, win, and fail conditions
- **Visual Effects**: Particle effects for collisions and collections

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| W / ↑ | Accelerate Forward |
| S / ↓ | Decelerate / Reverse |
| A / ← | Turn Left |
| D / → | Turn Right |
| SPACE | Boost (consumes extra energy) |
| P / ESC | Pause / Resume |
